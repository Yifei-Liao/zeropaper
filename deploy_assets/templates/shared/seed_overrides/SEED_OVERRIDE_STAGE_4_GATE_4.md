### Seeded-mode override (applies because `seeded: true` in `pipeline_state.json`)

The aggregate score and `ADVANCE / REVISE / MAJOR REWORK / ABANDON` label are diagnostic in seeded mode; they do not route the run. The seed fixed the research direction, and the preceding math/design/empirical gates already own correctness.

- Read `output/stage4/triage_vN.md` as well as both scorer reports. If the triage contains a `[FIX]` item that identifies a **specific correctness or rigor defect**, or either scorer identifies such a defect, return that claim to its existing owning audit. Forward only the defect and its cited evidence; neither triage nor scorer assertion overrules the audit. Each owning audit writes its report to a versioned path under `output/stage4/` (for example `output/stage4/g4_route_v{theory_version}/<its usual report name>`), never to its fixed Stage 3a report path; a return names those paths in its diagnostic, and Stage 3a's "Audit carry-forward" lists them as `INTERIM_AUDIT_REPORTS` when the rebuild re-fires the data auditors. A strategic, framing, novelty, importance, scope, fertility, or format suggestion does not become a correctness defect merely because triager assigned `[FIX]`. Follow the owning gate's normal seeded/faithful repair-or-halt route, under the correctness-return cap below.
- **Correctness-return cap.** An owning audit that confirms a forwarded defect repairs it upstream — a spec or theory mutate through Gate 2, a Stage 3a rebuild, and everything between there and this gate again — so each return buys a full campaign, and nothing else counts them (`loops.stage3a_upstream_return` resets at the acceptance that precedes this gate, `spec_audit_revision` at Gate 2's). Before leaving this gate on any such route, increment `loops.gate4_correctness_return.round` (Loop Registry; created on first reference) and commit that write; below its cap, take the owning gate's route as written. A challenge every owning audit rejects increments nothing and proceeds to Stage 5 as below. The counter resets only when this gate advances to Stage 5 or on the fresh-theory identity reset — never on the `theory_version` bump its own route causes (`core.md` exception 3). **At the cap**, do not return upstream again. The forwarded challenges still go to their owning audits — the audits decide what is a defect, never the scorer or the triager — and what they confirm decides the route: (a) an owning audit confirms a defect in a load-bearing claim — its own FAIL verdict, or a confirmed finding at severity ≥ 7 where the audit grades severity (the triager's own `[FIX]` threshold) — then halt: write the confirmed findings, every prior return's diagnostic, and the audit reports to `output/seed/gate4_correctness_halt.md`, set `pipeline_state.json:status = "halted_seed_gate4_correctness"` in the same step, commit both, and stop. A load-bearing defect that survives that many full repair campaigns is the design question the operator owns, and correctness is the one thing seeded mode never trades for fidelity. (b) Every confirmed finding is below that bar — proceed to Stage 5 carrying each one visibly: record it in `output/seed/limitations.md` with the audit that confirmed it, so it reaches the paper as a `[LIMITS]` item through the forwarding below. Nothing an audit confirmed is dropped on either branch.
